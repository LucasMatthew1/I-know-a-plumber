# I Know A Plumber - Design System & Brand Identity

## 1. Brand Concept & Aesthetic
- **Brand Name:** I Know A Plumber
- **Tone:** Professional, dependable, clean, friendly, straightforward, and trade-certified reliability.
- **Industry:** Modern plumbing, architectural fixtures sourcing, municipal permit processing, estimating/bidding, and contractor project support.
- **Audience:** Homeowners, general contractors, remodelers, property managers, and commercial project managers.

## 2. Color Palette (Matched to Official Logo)
- **Deep Trade Blue (Primary):** `#003C7A` / `#002F60` (HSL 212 90% 22%) — Represents reliability, code compliance, strength, and enterprise credibility.
- **Logo Precision Blue (Accent):** `#0060C0` (HSL 210 100% 38%) — Matches the official logo hue; represents clean water flow, fixture precision, modern service, and key interactive CTAs.
- **Clean Ice Wash (Subtle BG):** `#F0F7FE` & `#F8FAFC` — High whitespace, breathable layout, and crisp readability.
- **Charcoal Text (Foreground):** `#0F172A` — Optimal high-contrast readability (passes AAA standards).
- **Border / Structure:** `#E2E8F0` & `#DBEAFE` — Clean thin borders for cards and tables.

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Display:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weights: 600, 700, 800) — Bold, geometric, legible, and modern.
- **Body & Interface:** `Inter` (weights: 400, 500, 600) — Clean clarity, optimal readability on all screen resolutions.
