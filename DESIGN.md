# Ramos Plumbing Services - Design System & Brand Identity

## 1. Brand Concept & Aesthetic
- **Tone:** Professional, dependable, clean, straightforward, and trade-certified reliability.
- **Industry:** Modern plumbing, architectural fixtures, municipal permit processing, estimating/bidding, and project support.
- **Audience:** Homeowners, general contractors, remodelers, property managers, and commercial project managers.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Deep Navy (Primary):** `#0D3155` (HSL 210 75% 20%) — Represents reliability, code compliance, strength, and enterprise credibility.
- **Precision Blue (Accent):** `#0284C7` (HSL 199 89% 46%) — Represents clean water flow, fixture precision, modern service, and key interactive CTAs.
- **Clean Ice Wash (Subtle BG):** `#F0F9FF` & `#F8FAFC` — High whitespace, breathable layout, and crisp readability.
- **Charcoal Text (Foreground):** `#0F172A` — Optimal high-contrast readability (passes AAA standards).
- **Border / Structure:** `#E2E8F0` — Clean thin borders for cards and tables.

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Display:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weights: 600, 700, 800) — Bold, geometric, legible, and modern.
- **Body & Interface:** `Inter` (weights: 400, 500, 600) — Clean clarity, optimal readability on all screen resolutions.

## 4. Logo & Identity
- **Monogram & Mark:** Geometric precision badge combining interconnected industrial plumbing pipes and water flow dynamics.
- **Wordmark:** "RAMOS PLUMBING SERVICES" & "Plumbing & Project Support".
